Red lentil soup

Serves 4 · 35 minutes · Vegan

Ingredients

  • 250 g red lentils
  • 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 lemon

Method

  1. Soften the onion and carrots in olive oil.
  2. Add garlic, cumin and lentils, then 1.2 l of stock.
  3. Simmer 20 minutes, blend, and finish with lemon.

Tip: a spoon of tahini makes it creamier.
